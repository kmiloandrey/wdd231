Non Profit Membership

Benefits include:

  • Official listing in the Springfield Chamber Directory
  • Access to monthly community networking mixers
  • Invitations to annual non-profit workshops and training

COST: Free

Bronze Membership

Benefits include:

  • All Non Profit Membership benefits
  • Invitations to special Chamber luncheons and business expos
  • 10% discount on Chamber training seminars and workshops
  • Business ribbon-cutting ceremony promotion

COST: $50 annual

Silver Membership

Benefits include:

  • All Bronze Membership benefits
  • Rotating advertising spotlight position on the Home Page
  • 25% discount on event tickets and booth rentals
  • Featured social media shoutout once per quarter

COST: $100 annual

Gold Membership

Benefits include:

  • All Silver Membership benefits
  • Priority Home Page Spotlight placement
  • VIP invitations to exclusive Chamber leadership events
  • 50% discount on all Chamber events, sponsorships, and trainings
  • Dedicated banner ad in the monthly Springfield newsletter

COST: $150 annual
